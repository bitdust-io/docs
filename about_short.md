Personal information is becoming more valuable and significant in the rapidly developing digital world. Personal photos, files, data on relatives and children, etc. are currently stored separately in various centralized services and are often used without the permission of the data owner, sometimes even causing harm. The lack of secure storage is a problem for Internet users.

BitDust Technology solves this problem by giving users the ability to store their data fully encrypted on a decentralized network via an easy-to-use application, and to share data only with trusted contacts. At its core, BitDust is a distributed network for data storage and secure communications: by default each network participant provides a portion of their hard drive for other users and, at the same time, can store data on other peers. Either of these two roles can be disabled in the settings. Thanks to the redundancy of the backups, if someone loses your data, you can rebuild what was lost and give it to someone else to hold. And all of this happens without you having to do a thing - the BitDust software running on your device keeps your data safe.





Finally, you can take full control of your personal data on the Internet and make sure you are not compromised and your data is not sold to marketing companies.

BitDust is a distributed on-line storage network where only the data owner can read the data, and decides in advance which data can be shared with trusted contacts.

All files uploaded via BitDust are stored in small redundant pieces on other users' machines around the world. To be able to download your data back you must possess the private key that was used to encrypt the data during uploading.

Another user holding pieces of your data knows that this data belongs to you, but cannot read it without your private key - which gives you complete confidentiality of data storage. By design, in BitDust no one is able to read your messages or files, even if they were intercepted - even the developers of BitDust do not have access to your data.

The BitDust team stands for private, safe and truly confidential online communications for everyone. We have chosen our mission in the 21st century - to build a software product that will enable people around the world to have full control of their own digital lives.

How can you use BitDust?

You can install BitDust software on your personal computer or mobile device.

To register in the system you do not need to specify a mobile phone number or email address - BitDust does not collect any personal data, you are known in the network only by a nickname you choose.

With the BitDust app you can chat with your friends or business partners confidentially.

You can send photos, documents, and other files to each other.
